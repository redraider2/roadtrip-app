Kickoff Miles Picks: eight-city pilot
Prepared October 2, 2026

Scope
Lubbock, Tempe, Houston, San Antonio, Austin, Columbia (South Carolina), College Station/Bryan and Waco. Houston has separate UH and Rice inventories: nine venue destinations and 54 entries total.

Behavior
Trip HQ, Stay & Itinerary and Game Weekend display a separate Kickoff Miles Picks section after existing partner surfaces. Each venue gets six recommendations/resources. Unknown venues receive no picks. Away and neutral-site games do not inherit the selected team's home-city content. An active trip's venue takes precedence over a separate game selection for these picks.

Partner handling
The Culinary Gangster Tempe four-placement campaign and Cactus Theater inventory are unchanged. Picks do not enter the partner advertising event store. These editorial entries do not imply payment, sponsorship or an agreement with the featured businesses. Existing house ads remain available for sales.

Sources and copy
48 entries retain source checks from October 1, 2026. Six Columbia entries were checked October 2 against operator, university and official destination sources. Columbia Craft's website currently describes distribution/product-finder activity without confirming a visitor taproom, so the draft brewery outing was replaced by Riverbanks Zoo & Garden. Mast is categorized as shopping. Each entry retains source URLs and check dates in src/data/destinationPicks.json. These are basic operation/description checks, not guarantees of travel-date availability.

Seasonal handling
Santa's Wonderland stays explicitly labeled seasonal, with a November opening note and a Check seasonal dates link. It is not presented as currently open. Travelers must check the operator calendar.
